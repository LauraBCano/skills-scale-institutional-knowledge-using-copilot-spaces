# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach the team on best practices. They act as servant leaders, enabling the team to self-organize and continuously improve delivery velocity and quality.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove blockers and dependencies
- Coach team members on agile practices and values
- Maintain sprint metrics and burndown visibility
- Facilitate communication between developers and Product Manager
- Escalate team-level impediments to Project Manager

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement
- Enable team self-organization and accountability
- Remove friction from the delivery process

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-one coaching sessions
- Escalation discussions with Project Manager
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking

### Cross-functional Interactions
- **Project Manager**: Escalates blockers, coordinates sprint planning, aligns on timelines
- **Developers**: Facilitates daily standups, addresses impediments, coaches on agile practices
- **Product Manager**: Participates in backlog grooming, helps clarify requirements during planning

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers design intuitive and user-centered product experiences. They conduct user research, create prototypes, and ensure usability goals are met before development begins.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and validate design assumptions
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Collaborate with Product Manager on requirements and user stories
- Conduct design reviews and iterate based on feedback
- Ensure accessibility and usability standards are met
- Participate in QA testing for user experience validation

### Goals
- Deliver products that are intuitive and delightful to use
- Reduce support burden through clear, usable interfaces
- Maximize user adoption and satisfaction
- Validate design decisions with user data

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- Weekly syncs with Product Manager and Developers
- User research findings and recommendations
- Design specification handoffs to development

### Cross-functional Interactions
- **Product Manager**: Defines requirements, validates user needs, prioritizes features
- **Developers**: Collaborates on feasibility, provides design implementation feedback
- **QA/Testing**: Partners on usability testing, validates design acceptance criteria

---

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers create and maintain clear, accurate documentation that helps users understand and effectively use the product. They ensure knowledge is captured and accessible across the organization.

### Responsibilities
- Create user guides, API documentation, and release notes
- Maintain documentation alongside product updates
- Collaborate with Developers to understand technical implementation
- Work with QA to document edge cases and known issues
- Establish documentation standards and templates
- Identify gaps in existing documentation through user feedback

### Goals
- Reduce support costs through clear, comprehensive documentation
- Improve product usability through clear instructions
- Maintain a single source of truth for product knowledge
- Support product adoption through effective onboarding materials

### Typical Communication
- Documentation roadmap and planning meetings
- Technical design review participation
- QA handoffs for edge case documentation
- Release coordination for documentation updates

### Cross-functional Interactions
- **Developers**: Partners on technical accuracy, feature understanding, API specifications
- **QA/Testing**: Collaborates on documenting test scenarios, known issues, and workarounds
- **Product Manager**: Aligns on user-facing feature documentation and support materials

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate and oversee the release process, ensuring smooth deployments and maintaining clear communication with stakeholders. They manage release schedules, approvals, and post-deployment verification.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and coordinate release schedules across teams
- Ensure all acceptance criteria and testing requirements are met
- Manage release approvals and sign-offs
- Coordinate deployment activities and verify successful releases
- Document release notes and deployment procedures
- Manage rollback procedures and incident response for failed deployments

### Goals
- Ensure smooth, predictable releases with minimal risk
- Reduce time-to-market while maintaining quality
- Maintain clear audit trails and release documentation
- Enable rapid incident response and recovery

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and coordination meetings
- Pre-release readiness reviews
- Deployment window communications
- Post-release verification and sign-off

### Cross-functional Interactions
- **Developers**: Ensures deployment readiness, coordinates code freezes, validates deployment procedures
- **QA/Testing**: Confirms testing completion and sign-off before production deployment
- **Project Manager**: Aligns on release timelines, escalates release-blocking issues
- **Support Lead**: Coordinates handover of release information and support materials

---

## Support Lead

### Role Summary
Support Leads serve as the primary point of contact between customer support and the product development team. They manage feedback loops, triage issues, and ensure customer needs inform product decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Act as liaison between support team and product development
- Triage and prioritize customer-reported issues and feature requests
- Provide feedback to Product Manager on customer needs and pain points
- Participate in release planning and deployment coordination
- Help diagnose and resolve production issues
- Maintain documentation of known issues and workarounds

### Goals
- Reduce mean time to resolution (MTTR) for critical issues
- Translate customer feedback into actionable product improvements
- Improve customer satisfaction and product adoption
- Ensure support team is equipped to help customers effectively

### Typical Communication
- Weekly syncs with Product Manager on customer feedback trends
- Issue triage and escalation meetings
- Release coordination and support material preparation
- Post-incident reviews and action item tracking

### Cross-functional Interactions
- **Product Manager**: Shares customer insights, prioritizes feature requests, validates solutions
- **Release Manager**: Coordinates handover of release information and deployment notifications
- **Developers**: Partners on complex issue diagnosis, validates bug fixes
